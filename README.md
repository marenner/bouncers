# Bouncers

![Recording of bouncers being spawned and moving around the screen](./game.gif)

An exercise to learn about functions and classes.

Press A to spawn a bouncer with random speed and direction at a varying y-position.

Most bouncers move at a constant speed and bounce off of the edges of the screen. One bouncer is always at the average position of all other bouncers.
